# Rofly privacy policy

Version 5 · Last updated: 18 October 2026

## The short version

Rofly has no accounts, doesn't ask for an email or password, shows no ads and
sends no usage statistics. Your replays stay on your computer.

The only thing that leaves your computer is what **you** choose to share. What
other users share with their own copies is their decision.

## What data is handled

| Data | Where it comes from | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Folder paths, language and settings | you choose them | **only on your computer, never shared** |
| Names of the ten players, champions, result, KDA, items, patch, server, date and duration of a match | from the `.rofl` file header | your computer; if you share the match, Rofly users who search for it can also see it |
| The replay file | your replays folder | your computer; if you share the match, another user can download it from your PC |
| Your IP address | your internet connection | seen by the other computers on the Rofly network that your program connects to |
| A program identification key | created at install time | used to sign what you share; it doesn't say who you are, but lets what one installation shares be recognised |
| The connection code | randomly generated | announced on the network so another computer can find you; it changes every 24 hours |

## How the network works

Rofly has no central server. It works as a network between its users'
computers: searches and downloads go directly from one computer to another.
That's why, while the program is open, other computers on the network can see
your IP address, which roughly indicates where you connect from and who your
internet provider is.

Your program only hands over the content of replays you shared. There is no
listing of your files, and no other file on your disk can be requested.

## Public and private mode

At install time, and later in Settings, you choose whether what you catalogue
is shared.

- **Public:** the matches you catalogue can be found by searching.
- **Private:** nothing you catalogue shows up in other people's searches.
  Sharing becomes a decision you make one match at a time.

Switching to private doesn't withdraw what you had already shared, and doesn't
delete copies other people already downloaded.

## Players who don't use Rofly

A replay names the ten players in the match. When a user shares it, those
names and performances become searchable by other users, even though those
people agreed to nothing.

Any named player can ask for their matches to stop appearing by writing to the
contact below, without installing anything or giving a reason.

## What others share

The same match can be on the computer of any of the ten players, and a replay
you share can end up with whoever downloaded it. If another player, a friend
or anyone else, known or unknown, shares that match or your replay, they do so
by their own decision. Rofly and its developer don't control those actions,
aren't responsible for them, and can't delete what another person already
downloaded or shared.

Whoever publishes something is responsible for it. Section 8 of the terms
covers this in more detail.

## Third-party services

- **Riot Games (Data Dragon):** champion, item and rune images and names are
  downloaded from Riot's public server. Riot sees your IP address when
  serving them.
- **GitHub:** the version installed outside the Microsoft Store checks for
  updates on GitHub, which sees your IP address.
- **Microsoft Store:** the Store version is downloaded and updated through
  Microsoft, under Microsoft's privacy policy.

## What isn't collected

No emails, passwords, payment details or Riot account data are requested or
stored. Rofly doesn't use the Riot API, doesn't read the game's memory and
doesn't open the match content: it only reads the file header. There are no
ads, no usage statistics and no automatic error reports.

## Deletion

Your replays are in the folders you chose: delete them and nothing is left
locally. When uninstalling, Rofly asks whether to delete the settings too.
What another person already downloaded stays on their computer and Rofly
can't delete it.

## Changes to this policy

Rofly may add new features in the future, such as user accounts, ads or usage
statistics. If that happens, we will update this policy **before** the change
takes effect and announce it inside the program. Where the law requires it, we
will ask for your consent. If you don't agree with the changes, you can stop
using Rofly and delete your data.
